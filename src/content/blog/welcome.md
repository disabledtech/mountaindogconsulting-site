Welcome to Mountain Dog Consulting.

I'm Taylor Danielson. I consult on assistive technology and speak publicly
about disability, accessibility, and the technology that bridges the two.
The [about page](/about/) covers what I do and how to hire me. This page
is something different: a place to write about the work itself.

## What you'll find here

Most posts will fall into one of three buckets.

**Projects** — write-ups of things I've built, configured, or rolled out.
Voice assistants and smart-home gear that actually fits how I live, computer
access setups, environmental controls. Some will be successes; some will be
careful retrospectives of what didn't work and why.

**Devices and tools** — practical reviews of the assistive technology I use,
recommend, or have ruled out. Less "5 stars" and more "here's what this is
good for, here's what it isn't, here's who should consider it." The hardware
landscape moves fast and the published reviews are usually thin.

**Tools of thought** — occasional posts about how I think about the work:
the gap between catalogue-aware and adoption-aware recommendations, why most
assistive-tech rollouts leave drawers full of unused devices, what changes
when the person using the technology is also the one specifying it.

## Why blog-first?

A consulting practice is partly credentialing. You can hire someone for their
résumé, or you can hire them for their writing about the work — the second is
a lot easier to evaluate before the engagement. The posts here are the
homework I'd do anyway; making them public means they justify themselves.

## A small accessibility note

Every post on this site is built to the same standards as the rest of it:
WCAG 2.2 AA, screen-reader tested, no required JavaScript, careful with
contrast and motion. If something here doesn't work for you, please write
to [accessibility@mountaindogconsulting.com](mailto:accessibility@mountaindogconsulting.com)
or read the full [accessibility statement](/accessibility/).
